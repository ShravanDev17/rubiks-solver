Rubik's Cube Solver

dev by Shravan S

Pick a color, then click stickers to paint them. Centers are fixed.

How to enter your cube
  • Hold the cube with the white center on top and the green center facing you.
  • Front, Right, Back, Left: look straight at each face with the white side up.
  • Up (white): look from above, with the green side toward the bottom of your view.
  • Down (yellow): look from below, with the green side toward the top of your view.

Drag to rotate the view.
